Care close to home

A dental clinic rooted in the local community.

Horcabas Dental Clinic serves patients and families in Oroquieta City and nearby communities in Misamis Occidental. Our clinic supports preventive, general, restorative, cosmetic, and family dental needs from a convenient location in Lower Langcangan.

Good care starts with a clear understanding of the patient. During a visit, share any symptoms, health conditions, medicines, previous dental experiences, and concerns about treatment. The dentist can then assess your needs and explain appropriate options.

Recommendations are personal. No website can determine which treatment is right for you, which is why our online information is designed to help you prepare for a conversation rather than replace an examination.
